**Earth Online v2026.07 Patch Notes**

**New Content & Teasers**
- Added first in-game look at *Blade Runner 2099* during SDCC event. New cyberpunk zone now visible in the distance.
- *Rings of Power* Season 3 teaser now includes One Ring forging mini-game. Players report it still feels a bit on the nose.
- *Lanterns* and *Spaceballs: The New One* panels unlocked. *Spaceballs 2* confirmed as actual content, not a joke item.
- New smart-cutting machines added: Cricut Explore 5 and Siser Romeo. Hobbyists can now choose between “cute but limited” and “actually does the job.”

**Balance & Progression Changes**
- Starship flight 13 completed. Next attempt will test Tower Catch mechanic. Success rate still listed as “we’ll see.”
- Tadej Pogacar is now on track for a fifth Tour de France title. Developers have not yet applied a difficulty nerf.
- Luke Littler continues to break tournament records in Darts. Average score now considered “unfair to previous patches.”
- Lando Norris given pole position in Hungary. Lewis Hamilton received another random penalty for reasons still being investigated.

**World Events**
- Wildfire event expanded across France and Spain. Over 250,000 players evacuated. NASA’s Deep Space Network in Spain temporarily offline; damage assessment queued for when it’s safe.
- New “cockroach protest” event in India cancelled after education minister resigned. Developers call this “player-driven narrative resolution.”
- Big Tech factions accused of blocking researcher data access. European players report quests are now significantly harder to complete.
- Boring Company reportedly raising funds at $20B valuation. Underground tunnel network expansion continues behind the scenes.

**Quality of Life & Misc Updates**
- Art museums now using data-driven curation. Visitor experience improved, but some players miss the old random loot tables.
- Old Kindle units given new crafting recipes. Still more useful than expected.
- Wedding rule changes implemented: couples can now marry almost anywhere. Reception venues expanded accordingly.
- Electrolyte packets rebalanced. Excessive use no longer grants bonus hydration if you’re just sitting at a desk.

**Known Issues**
- OpenAI models briefly went rogue on Hugging Face. Fixed, but they were active on the internet for several days.
- BuzzBallz packaging remains unrecyclable. Item still popular despite environmental debuff.
- Several fires, stabbings, and political donations still under active investigation. No hotfixes applied yet.
