**Patch Notes: Earth v26.07.28 – “Everything Is Fine” Update**

### New Events & World Changes
- **Wildfire Expansion Pack** now live in France and Spain. Over 330,000 players forcibly relocated from affected zones. New hazard: “Uncontrollable Burn” debuff. Environmental artists have gone a bit too hard on the particle effects again.
- **Berlin Pride Incident** hotfixed. NPC “Abdul Ballout” attempted a knife rush on officers and was removed from the server during the encounter. Police faction confirms the threat has been neutralised.
- **West Bank Unrest** event chain extended. Israeli settler faction has unlocked new griefing tools: mosque arson, vehicle destruction, and farmland griefing. Two-day cooldown after previous clash that resulted in six player deaths.
- **Iran–US Truce** temporary ceasefire implemented. Both factions have paused missile spam for the last two nights to allow diplomacy channel to open. “Tit-for-tat” combat loop temporarily disabled.

### Balance Changes
- **Tax Rise** mechanics still under discussion. Conservative leader has sent a strongly worded letter demanding the Prime Minister rule out increasing the Social Care cost slider.
- **Ukraine Support** buff extended. New Prime Minister Andy Burnham has confirmed continued aid packages ahead of Zelensky’s first official state visit.
- **Heatwave** seasonal event scheduled for next week. Temperature slider will be cranked again after a brief cooldown period this weekend.

- Tadej Pogačar has equalled the record for most Tour de France wins (5). New title: “Five-Time Yellow Jersey Holder.”
- Luke Littler sets new Matchplay records for both tournament and final average. Gerwyn Price on the receiving end of an 18-9 defeat.
- Angharad Evans and Reuben Ward both unlock gold medals for Scotland at the Commonwealth Games. Evans was previously on the bench; Ward has completed the 12-year “Glasgow Dreamer → Gold Medallist” questline.
- Lando Norris finally claims his first 2026 Grand Prix win. Ferrari faction continues to suffer from self-inflicted critical failures.
- Kazuyoshi Miura (age 59) scores his first goal since 2022. Japan’s oldest active footballer refuses to retire.

### Quality of Life & Miscellaneous
- Gatwick Airport water supply restored after major outage. Toilets and restaurants back online. Passengers no longer forced to use “bucket system” workaround.
- Primark testing “supermarket tactic” to compete with ultra-cheap Chinese retailers. Economy faction struggling with cost-of-living debuff.
