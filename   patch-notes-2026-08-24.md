**Patch Notes: Reality 2.0 – “Everything’s Fine” Update**

**Vehicles**
- GM eBoost brake-by-wire system now correctly fails to stop the car in 22 documented crashes. This is a feature, not a bug. Players are advised to treat the brake pedal as a polite suggestion.
- Volkswagen Passat collision physics have been tuned for extra lethality on the A66 motorway. Two police officers have been removed from the active roster.

**Audio & Privacy**
- Added inaudible sound-based browser fingerprinting. AliExpress now tracks you across tabs even if you mute the game. Yes, it’s outdated. Yes, we still shipped it.

**Collectibles & Hazards**
- Mummified remains added to the in-game marketplace. They now carry toxic spores that cause biodeterioration. No safety tooltips were added because “buyer beware” is funnier.

**Tech & Smuggling**
- Nvidia senior manager has been indicted for running an AI server smuggling ring to China. Supermicro now has a black-market vendor tab that occasionally gets you banned in real life.

**Sports**
- F1 Dutch Grand Prix: Teammates are now officially the highest-priority target. Friendly fire between teammates is no longer punished.
- Keely Hodgkinson has withdrawn from the Diamond League due to “body not quite ready.” Stamina stat temporarily set to “nope.”

**Economy & Politics**
- Clean energy capacity increased by 45 GW despite attempted nerfs. Solar and wind now generate more power than intended.
- US-Canada trade war escalated. Auto tariffs have been applied to both sides. Players trading across the border will now pay twice and receive nothing.

**Horror & Crime**
- Snooker champion Graeme Dott has been found guilty of child sex abuse. His character model has been removed from the pool/snooker minigame.
- Wildfire event near Reno now requires 90,000 players to evacuate. The fire is completely uncontained and will probably spread to your base.

**Quality of Life**
- Graduate job vacancies reduced by ~50%. The “find employment” questline is now significantly harder and pays less.
- Chuwi Unibook laptop added as a budget PC option. It looks like a MacBook if you squint and have never seen a MacBook. Performance is… an experience.

**Known Issues**
- World ocean temperature has hit a new record. Water levels and El Niño events are behaving exactly as predicted and we’re still surprised for some reason.
- Several explosive devices were found in a teenager’s house. The “teenager with viable bombs” event is currently under investigation.

More patches coming soon. Probably.
