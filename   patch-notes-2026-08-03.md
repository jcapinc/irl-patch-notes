**Reality 1.26 - "August Hotfix" Patch Notes**

**New Features**
- Added Cyborg Cockroach Diving Suits. Cockroaches can now explore underwater ruins with tiny rebreathers. Early reports indicate they’re already better at not panicking than most players.
- Deployed the Hubble Space Telescope as a buildable desktop model. Now you can own the universe in minifigure scale while the real one continues to judge your life choices from orbit.
- Introduced the 2026 Jetta Sport trim. The “cheap car” class has not been fully deprecated. Top speed remains “adequate,” handling is “what you’d expect for the price.”

**Balance Changes**
- Headlights have been made significantly brighter and whiter. Visibility increased for drivers; blindness debuff for everyone else has been massively buffed.
- Sperm whales now blow bubbles when sleepy. This is apparently normal and not a new emote.
- Early release scheme has been patched to exclude rapists and child sex offenders. Several high-profile NPCs remain on the old build for now.

**Bug Fixes & Known Issues**
- AI-supervised remote exam system has been temporarily disabled after it awarded god-tier scores to 58,000 students. Retake event scheduled. Top score multiplier has been hotfixed from 5× to “please just do the exam again.”
- SpaceX has purchased 130,000 acres of Louisiana marshland. New launch site added. Local wildlife has received the “relocation” debuff.
- Trump’s DOJ has proposed removing the public’s ability to sue polluters. Environmental enforcement mechanics may be moving to single-player only.

**Miscellaneous**
- Betelgeuse has gained a companion star. Stargazers report the red supergiant is no longer flying solo.
- Several rivers (Rhine, Danube, Po) have had their water levels drastically reduced. Barge transport and hydroelectric output have both been nerfed.
- Infantino’s FIFA re-election campaign is experiencing growing player revolt. England and Wales factions are threatening non-cooperation.

**Notes**
- July was the driest on record for England and Wales. The weather system continues to run in “aggressive summer” mode.
- Several high-profile NPCs (columnists, comedians, authors) have been removed from the server this month. Memorial events active.
