**World Update v26.8 – Patch Notes**

**Bug Fixes**
- Fixed an issue where two firefighting helicopters could occupy the same airspace, resulting in an unintended mid-air collision. Two crew members have been removed from the active roster.
- Resolved a physics exploit allowing players to transport human remains inside luggage. The suitcase meta has been heavily nerfed.

**Balance Changes**
- Small boat crossings have received increased enforcement. Players attempting the crossing now have a significantly higher chance of being intercepted. Additional “safe route” spawns added to the map.
- Prolonged drought event extended. Crop yields reduced across multiple regions. Farmers’ Union faction is now demanding government intervention before the food shortage debuff spreads.
- Israeli strike frequency increased on Gaza City, Khan Younis, and Deir el-Balah after Hamas accepted a disarmament deal. Several military operative NPCs removed from the map.

**Quality of Life**
- Ariana Grande has entered a visibility cooldown at the end of her world tour. Expect reduced public appearances until further notice.
- Glasgow 2026 Commonwealth Games concluded successfully, but the future of the entire game mode remains uncertain. No guarantees on continued support.

**New Features**
- Added “Loneliness Influencer” career path. Players can now pursue solitude as an aspirational lifestyle while loudly insisting they are not lonely.
- Introduced ransom note forensic linguistics system. Short written messages can now reveal surprising amounts of information about the writer.

**Known Issues**
- Multiple trackside fires still causing train delays between London and Stansted. Workarounds are being investigated.
- Indonesia ferry fire event has left 5 dead and 41 missing. Respawn rates currently disabled for affected passengers.
- Global memory shortage is now affecting MacBook Air stock. Players may experience reduced RAM during character creation.
