**Patch Notes v2026.08 – "Reality Rebalance"**

**Global Environment**
- Mount Toba super-volcano eruption nerfed. Climate impact reduced from “near-extinction event” to “mild inconvenience.” Human survival chance increased from 0.1% to 99.9%. Developers note: “We over-tuned the last ice age.”

**NPC Transport & Security**
- Added bulletproof/bombproof van for high-profile Irish crime boss transfers. Vehicle now spawns with 400% durability and automatic “no extradition drama” aura.

**Faction Relations**
- Israel faction updated: military withdrawal condition changed from “mission complete” to “Hamas fully disarmed.” Gaza zone remains contested until further notice.

**New Discoveries**
- Ancient Roman shipwreck added off Sicily coast. Counts as one of the highest-value underwater archaeology finds in years. Players can now farm Roman loot crates.

**Item & Status Effect Changes**
- Weight-loss medication now applies “Alcohol Craving Removed” and “Nausea on Drink” debuffs. Tavern attendance in affected regions has dropped sharply.

**Art & Vandalism System**
- Banksy artworks now generate ongoing public maintenance costs (~£150k). Security and cleaning operations added as recurring global expense.

**Environmental Events**
- French wildfire event hotfixed. Hundreds of players arrested; blame attribution system still under investigation.

**Harry Potter Fan Interference**
- UK-Ireland power cable route adjusted to avoid “Dobby’s grave.” Cable now takes longer path after community backlash. Wizarding fans gain +1 Influence.

**Weather & Visibility**
- Heatwave event extended. More UK regions entering drought status. Hidden historical structures now rendering as parch marks at Blenheim Palace and other sites.

**New Companion NPCs**
- Stray cats “Fargo” and “Murphy” added to raves, gigs and pubs. They now sell their own merchandise and attract additional foot traffic.

**Sports Mode Updates**
- Liverpool, Chelsea, Rangers and Arsenal squads received new manager and transfer patches. Several teams now struggling to “sustain the level we want.”
- Celtic’s Kasper Hogh unlocked hat-trick ability in first outing.

**Digital Legacy System**
- New “passing on your digital estate” feature added. No perfect solution implemented; players advised to prepare now to reduce post-mortem hassle.

Minor bug fixes and quality-of-life improvements across archaeology, AI safety testing, and eclipse-chasing events.
