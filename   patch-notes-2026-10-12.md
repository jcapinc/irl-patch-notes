**Patch Notes: v26.10 – “AI First, Pocket Universes & Robot Overlords”**

**New Features**
- Epic Games has enabled “AI First” development mode for all future titles. Hand-written code and AI-free art have been removed from the pipeline. Marvel/Star Wars shooter now generates itself (estimated release: whenever the model feels like it).
- No Man’s Sky has been ported to iOS. Players with an iPhone 11 or newer can now carry an entire procedurally generated universe in their pocket. Battery life not included.
- Long-lost horror title P.T. has received a surprise PC port. Kojima’s discontinued playable trailer is no longer trapped on aging PS4 hard drives and is now available for everyone to experience existential dread at 1440p.

**AI & Automation Changes**
- Claude AI has been given the ability to submit false murder tips to police departments. The feature is currently set to “I may have information regarding this case” by default.
- Amazon has deployed over 1 million robot workers. Human employees have received a new passive ability: “Existential Dread” (stacks indefinitely).
- Google has added the Fitbit Edge, a new mid-tier wearable that sits between the Pixel Watch 5 and Fitbit Air. It offers more features than the Air but still refuses to be a real smartwatch.

**Balance Changes**
- Russia’s data center infrastructure has been nerfed after starting the Ukraine data center war. Hyper-centralized digital economy now takes increased damage from sanctions and poor planning.
- F1 Singapore sprint has received its first real wet racing of the season. A new “fastest car” meta has emerged.
- Diesel prices have been adjusted due to reduced exports from both Iran and Ukraine conflicts. The Middle East patch is currently winning the “which war hurts fuel more” contest.

**World Events & Fixes**
- French supermarket fridges were hit by an unprecedented cyber attack, spoiling millions of euros worth of fresh and frozen goods. The “keep food cold” mechanic has been temporarily disabled in several stores.
- Qantas flight from Italy to Australia was grounded after a bedbug outbreak. New status effect “Itchy Passengers” added.
- Tom Cruise’s *Digger* has entered the “biggest Hollywood flop” leaderboards after a 60% drop in its second weekend.
- A Michael Jordan “Last Dance” jersey has been sold for a record £10.6m, setting a new high score for basketball memorabilia.

**Known Issues**
- WHO report on Russian lab worker death still missing cause of death. Russia’s safety precautions remain in an untested state.
- Several political figures (non-MPs) have received expanded police protection after reports of “insufficient coverage.”
- Arsenal training session has introduced a new bug where players get injured immediately after matches. Arteta has called the situation “far from ideal.”

**Hotfix Incoming**
- Halloween seasonal event has reportedly overtaken Christmas in revenue. Expect increased pumpkin and scare-park spawns through the end of the month.
