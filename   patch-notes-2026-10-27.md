**Critical Security Update**
- Fixed a code-execution vulnerability in the UEFI boot process that allowed attackers to run arbitrary code before the OS even loaded. Most servers on the planet have received the emergency firmware flash. Players using legacy hardware may experience spontaneous early-game termination.

- Natural-language protein and genome design tools now accept “vibe-based” prompts. Tell the AI what kind of protein you want and it will attempt to generate it. Results may vary from “useful enzyme” to “accidental bioweapon.”

**AI & Simulation Improvements**
- DeepMind Genie 4 world model deployed to developers and Google AI Ultra subscribers. Features improved physics, more realistic humans, and audio. Expect NPCs to occasionally question their existence in more convincing ways.
